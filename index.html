WHO WE ARE

Ideas Into Impact.

The RRCE IEEE Student Branch is a student-driven technical community at RajaRajeswari College of Engineering, Bengaluru. It aims to encourage innovation, technical excellence, professional development and collaboration.

Through workshops, technical events, project exhibitions and knowledge-sharing sessions, students get opportunities to explore emerging technologies and develop practical skills.
